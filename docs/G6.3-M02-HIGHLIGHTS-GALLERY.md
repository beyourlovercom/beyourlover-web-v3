## 1. Role

M02 introduces 3–4 top product selling points immediately after M01.
Each card communicates one idea only; M02 is not a dense feature grid and does not replace M05 deep storytelling.

## 2. Naughty Clock Pink canonical cards

1. 2-in-1 Design
2. Cute, Yet Discreet
3. A Gift for Special Moments

Content and media come from the approved real Naughty Clock Pink product assets. AI-generated replacement product imagery is prohibited.
## 3. Layout contract

Desktop: horizontal large-card gallery; one primary card dominates the viewport and the next card peeks into view.
Tablet: preserve horizontal browsing with reduced card width; do not collapse to equal-width 3-column cards.
Mobile: one large card per swipe with partial next-card affordance where space allows.
Canonical stage keeps visible side gutters and centered section heading.

## 4. Interaction contract

Primary interaction is native/controlled horizontal scroll with explicit previous/next controls on desktop.
No autoplay by default.
Snap behavior is allowed; keyboard/focus navigation is required.
Reduced-motion must not change content accessibility.
Each card remains useful without JavaScript.
